01AgriTech · Product⚡ Vibe Coded

ThiruAgro — Intelligent Crop Guidance Platform

A mobile-accessible platform built to help smallholder farmers make informed crop decisions and detect plant diseases before they cause yield loss.

Problem
Farmers in rural Tamil Nadu lacked accessible, timely guidance on crop diseases. Extension officer visits are infrequent; poor decisions were costing livelihoods.
Solution
Image-based disease detection combined with a crop guidance layer that provides context-specific recommendations by season, soil type, and region. Subscription-based model for sustained access.
Business Value
Reduced crop loss decision latency from days to seconds. Designed for WhatsApp integration for zero-app-download adoption.
